Expression audio: offline sampler checks

Renders the bundled Salamander A4 recording through the bundled Tone.js sampler into memory. Checks velocity gain and the release envelope captured at attack. No sound is sent to speakers; no MIDI or browser settings are accessed.

For the simultaneous-note test, two MIDI keys use the same recording so the remaining ordinary voice can be compared sample for sample. This isolates envelope behavior; it is not a listening test or a complete trainer playback test.
